Google Play Data Safety Compliance

Account & Data Deletion Request

Application: ClutchClip — Instant Replay Screen Recorder • Created by Aditya • Version: v1.00.01

In accordance with Google Play Store User Data policies, users of ClutchClip (created by Aditya) can delete their linked Google Account profile and associated data at any time.

Option 1: Instant In-App Account Deletion

  1. Open the ClutchClip app on your device or at ClutchClip Web.
  2. Navigate to the Settings tab at the bottom right.
  3. Inside the Google Account & Profile card, tap Delete Account & Data and confirm.

Option 2: What Data is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Deleted Immediately: Your Firebase Authentication user profile (email, display name, UID), cloud-synced subscription verification record, and all local IndexedDB video highlights stored on your device.
  • Google Play Subscriptions: Deleting your ClutchClip account does not automatically cancel active billing cycles managed by Google Play. To avoid future charges, please also cancel any active subscription in Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
