Privacy Policy

Application: ClutchClip — Instant Replay Screen Recorder • Created by Aditya • Effective Date: March 30, 2026

Welcome to ClutchClip ("we," "our," or "us"), created and maintained by Aditya. This Privacy Policy explains how ClutchClip handles your information when you use our mobile application and web application.

1. Screen Recording & Audio Capture Data (Local-First Processing)

ClutchClip requires Android FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROJECTION (screen capture) and RECORD_AUDIO (microphone / internal audio) permissions solely to maintain a rolling flashback video buffer and record clips at your explicit command.

  • 100% On-Device Storage: All rolling replay buffers and saved video highlights are stored strictly locally on your device using IndexedDB and local device storage.
  • No Cloud Video Uploads: We never upload, view, transmit, or store your screen recordings or microphone audio on any external servers.

2. Account & Authentication Information

If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In or Email/Password authentication (powered by Firebase Authentication), we collect your basic profile details (name, email address, and account UID) solely to synchronize your subscription tier and account preferences across sessions.

3. Subscriptions & Google Play Billing

ClutchClip offers optional paid memberships (Premium — ₹29 and Premium Plus — ₹99). All payment transactions on Android are processed directly by Google Play Billing (Library v8.3.0). We do not collect, process, or store your credit card, UPI, or banking details.

4. Advertisements (Free Tier Only)

For users on the Free tier, ClutchClip displays banner and rewarded advertisements served via Google AdMob. Google AdMob may use device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) to serve relevant ads and enforce frequency capping. Subscribing to Premium or Premium Plus completely disables all advertisements.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You have complete control over your recorded clips and can delete individual highlights or clear the entire local storage vault at any time from the Highlight Vault or Settings tab. Uninstalling the app removes all locally cached recordings.

6. Children's Privacy

ClutchClip does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Policy & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or regulatory requirements. If you have questions regarding privacy or data handling, please contact the developer (Aditya) through the official Google Play Store listing support contact.
