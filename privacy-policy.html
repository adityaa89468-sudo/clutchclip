Official Privacy Policy

ClutchClip — Instant Game Replay Recorder

Package ID: com.adityaclutchclip994.app
Effective Date: October 23, 2026  |  Last Updated: October 23, 2026

Welcome to ClutchClip ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how ClutchClip collects, uses, processes, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application.

Core Privacy Guarantee: All screen recordings, circular replay buffers, and audio recordings captured by ClutchClip are processed and stored 100% locally on your device. We never upload, view, or transmit your recorded video clips or microphone audio to any external server.

1. Permissions & Sensitive Device Access

To function as an instant retroactive game replay recorder, ClutchClip requests the following user-initiated permissions:

  • Screen Capture & Media Projection (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROJECTION): Used exclusively when you explicitly tap "Start Replay Engine" and approve the Android system screen-cast prompt. The app maintains a temporary circular buffer in local device memory so you can save the last 15–60 seconds of gameplay. A persistent notification is always visible while active, and recording stops immediately when you stop the engine.
  • Microphone & Audio Recording (RECORD_AUDIO & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE): Used only if you enable Microphone or Dual Audio in Settings and start the Replay Engine to record your voice commentary alongside internal game audio. Audio is never recorded without your active consent and is stored strictly inside your local video clips.
  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Used to render the draggable floating "CLIP IT!" trigger button over your game so you can save replays with a single tap without leaving your game.
  • Local Storage / Media Access: Used to save exported MP4/WebM highlight clips into your device's local storage and Movies/ClutchClip gallery folder.

2. Information We Collect (Optional Account & Cloud Sync)

You can use ClutchClip in Guest Mode without creating an account. If you choose to sign in with Google, we use Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to store minimal account metadata:

  • Account Identifiers: Your Google display name, email address, profile photo URL, and unique Firebase User ID (UID).
  • App Preferences & Subscription Status: Your chosen recording settings (resolution, FPS, buffer duration), Pro membership status, and non-video clip statistics (such as total clip count).

We do not upload your actual video files to Cloud Firestore.

3. Advertising & Monetization

  • Google AdMob: The free version of ClutchClip displays banner and app-open advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's Advertising ID, coarse location, and diagnostic data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads in accordance with Google's Advertising Privacy Policy. Pro subscribers enjoy an ad-free experience.
  • Google Play Billing: In-app purchases and Pro subscriptions (Monthly or Yearly) are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We do not collect, process, or store your credit card, UPI, or banking details.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Data is shared only with essential service providers required to operate the app, encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Firestore)
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Services & Google Play Billing

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Local Video Clips: You have complete control over your saved clips. You can delete individual clips at any time inside the ClutchClip Gallery or by uninstalling the app.

Account & Cloud Data Deletion: If you signed in with Google and wish to permanently delete your account and all associated Firebase data, you can request immediate deletion by emailing us at adityaa89468@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My ClutchClip Account". All associated profile and Firestore records will be permanently erased within 7 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

ClutchClip is intended for general gaming audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal data without parental consent, we will delete it immediately.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
